Data sources and assumptions

Terrain: USGS Astrogeology Mars 2020 CTX DTM 20 m and orthomosaic 6 m; slope hazard from HiRISE DTM 1 m where it exists. Orbiters and Sun: JPL Horizons. Ground temperature: THEMIS night IR, TES albedo and the Horizons Sun through a ground heat model. Radiation: Curiosity RAD measured 0.67 mSv/day at Gale (22 g/cm² of air above); scaled here by each spot's air column (MOLA elevation, scale height assumed) and by how much sky the terrain leaves open. Suit speed (half Tobler) and drift (2 % of distance) are assumptions.
