About the data

Radiation map: Curiosity measured 0.67 mSv/day at Gale; other places are scaled by the air above them (elevation and an assumed scale height). When you plan a walk, the planner builds that area from MOLA 463 m elevation and real orbiter passes. Jezero opens in full detail (20 m). Globe elevation: MOLA-based shape model MarsTopo719, referenced to the GMM-3 areoid (SHTOOLS). Color: Celestia Mars mosaic. Heights on the globe are exaggerated; readouts are true values.
